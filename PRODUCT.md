# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary users are students preparing for the UMF Cluj admission exam. They study on desktop browsers and on phones through the web app, often working through demanding biology material that needs to be understood, reviewed, and remembered efficiently.

## Product Purpose

The website itself is the learning product. It rewrites and organizes biology lessons into a simpler, clearer study experience so students can understand difficult concepts faster, revise more efficiently, retain information longer, and study with less friction. Success means that a student can open a lesson, quickly understand how it is structured, and make meaningful progress without having to decode a dense textbook presentation.

## Positioning

A consistently structured biology study tool that makes the UMF Cluj admission curriculum easier to learn and creates a clear foundation for additional study features.

## Brand Personality

Understanding, motivating, and clear. The product should feel like a capable study companion: supportive without being childish, confident without becoming clinical, and concise without losing the detail students need.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic “AI slop” websites built from fashionable but interchangeable patterns. Avoid dense textbook pages that present long, undifferentiated walls of information and make students work to discover the lesson structure.

## Design Principles

1. **Make the lesson structure immediately legible.** Students should always understand where they are, what matters, and what comes next.
2. **Reduce the effort required to understand.** Break difficult biology into clear explanations and meaningful visual groupings without oversimplifying the subject.
3. **Support active revision and retention.** Organize content so students can scan, revisit, and reinforce important ideas efficiently.
4. **Keep the product motivating through visible progress.** Encourage continued study with clear states and useful feedback rather than distracting gamification.
5. **Build a consistent foundation for new study features.** New capabilities should extend the same predictable lesson vocabulary instead of introducing fragmented interaction patterns.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No project-specific accessibility standard or accommodation has been requested. Preserve sensible baseline usability, especially readable lesson content and comfortable interaction on phone-sized screens.
